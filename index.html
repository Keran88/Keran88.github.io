Biography

I am a PhD candidate in Information Technology at Deakin University, Australia. My research focuses on secure authentication for autonomous and distributed systems, integrating authentication protocols, applied cryptography, artificial intelligence and machine learning.

My doctoral research investigates lightweight mutual authentication, continuous authentication and machine learning-driven risk modelling for drone swarm networks. I also have academic teaching experience across cybersecurity, computer networks, programming and software engineering.
