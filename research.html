Doctoral Research

My PhD research at Deakin University focuses on developing intelligent and secure authentication frameworks by integrating machine learning-driven risk modelling with cryptographic authentication protocols.

Research Area Focus
Secure Authentication for Drone Swarm Networks Development of lightweight mutual authentication mechanisms for secure drone-to-drone communication in autonomous swarm environments.
Continuous Authentication for Drone Swarm Networks Development of continuous authentication mechanisms for maintaining trust between communicating drones beyond initial authentication.
AI-based Risk Modelling for Drone Swarm Networks Application of machine learning-driven risk modelling to support adaptive authentication decisions in autonomous distributed systems.
