Biography

I am a PhD candidate in Information Technology at Deakin University, Australia. My research focuses on secure authentication for autonomous and distributed systems, with particular interests in authentication protocols, applied cryptography, artificial intelligence and machine learning.

My doctoral research investigates lightweight mutual authentication, continuous authentication and machine learning-driven risk modelling for drone swarm networks.

My broader research interests include artificial intelligence, evolutionary computation, computational intelligence, cybersecurity, Internet of Things, computer networks, distributed systems, authentication protocols, applied cryptography and cyber-physical systems security.

Current Research

Secure Authentication for Drone Swarm Networks

Development of lightweight mutual authentication mechanisms for secure drone-to-drone communication in autonomous swarm environments.

Continuous Authentication for Drone Swarm Networks

Development of continuous authentication mechanisms for maintaining trust between communicating drones beyond initial authentication.

AI-based Risk Modelling for Drone Swarm Networks

Application of machine learning-driven risk modelling to support adaptive authentication decisions in autonomous distributed systems.
