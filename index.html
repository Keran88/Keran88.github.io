Biography

I am a PhD candidate in Information Technology at Deakin University, Australia.

My research focuses on lightweight mutual authentication, continuous authentication, and machine learning-driven risk modelling for drone swarm networks.

I’m passionate about cybersecurity research and teaching, particularly where applied cryptography, autonomous systems, and AI intersect. Alongside my research, I have taught across cybersecurity, computer networks, programming, and software engineering.
